How we worked this out. We assume {{ s.utilisation }}% of that capacity gets booked and paid, because no diary is full all year. Break-even = yearly costs ÷ realistic bookings. Floor = (yearly costs + yearly salary) ÷ realistic bookings. Recommended = floor + {{ s.buffer }}% for income tax, leave, slow months and reinvesting.
